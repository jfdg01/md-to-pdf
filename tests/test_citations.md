## Citations

With `citation_style: author-year`, in-text markers render as `(Author, year)`
instead of numeric `[n]`.

A single citation [@smith2020] and a grouped one [@brown2018; @lee2021] produce
author-year markers. The **References** section is ordered alphabetically by
author and lists only the cited entries.
